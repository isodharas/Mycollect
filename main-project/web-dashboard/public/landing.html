How it works

From sensor to action in seconds.

01
Sensor detects
MQ-135 reads methane, ammonia and H₂S every 15 minutes. HC-SR04 measures fill level ±1cm. IP65 weatherproof enclosure.
02
Cloud receives
NodeMCU transmits TLS-encrypted JSON via MQTT to AWS IoT Core. 24-hour local buffer during WiFi outages.
03
ML classifies
Lambda runs Random Forest on 5 features. Assigns LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL with a health risk score 0–100.
04
Action taken
Dashboard updates live. CRITICAL bins trigger push notifications. Routes reprioritise by health risk, not calendar.
Capabilities

Every decision driven by health risk.

Machine Learning 01
Random Forest Classifier
100 decision trees. 296 training samples. 95.94% cross-validation accuracy. Classifies every bin every 15 minutes — automatically.
Health-First Design 02
Gas weighted 70%, Fill 30%
A bin at 40% capacity emitting 400 PPM ranks higher than a full bin with clean air. Because in Sri Lanka's tropical climate, the gas is the threat — not the overflow.
IoT Hardware 03
NodeMCU + MQ-135
Methane, ammonia and H₂S detection 50–1000 PPM. IP65 weatherproof. TLS-encrypted MQTT to AWS.
Cloud Backend 04
Serverless on Lambda
ProcessBinData + GetBinData in ap-southeast-2. DynamoDB dual-table. 446ms — eleven times faster than target.
Mobile App 05
Flutter + Sinhala
Real-time alerts. Collection schedules. Sinhala language support for every Sri Lankan citizen.

The moment that made this necessary

"On 14 April 2017, a 300-foot waste mountain in Colombo collapsed — killing 32 people and destroying 145 homes. Gas had been building for decades. No system existed to detect it."

The warning signs were there for years. Odour complaints, structural concerns, visible emissions. There was simply no infrastructure to monitor, quantify, or escalate them. MyCollect is that infrastructure — built in Sri Lanka, for Sri Lanka.
